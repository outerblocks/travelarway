# Regulatory Requirements by Country — Tanzania First Pass

**Cut-off:** 22 October 2026  
**Scope:** OuterBlocks Destinations Limited is the intended Travelar operating entity under parent OuterBlocks Limited, subject to documentary verification; Travelar is a six-mode platform (bus, ferry, flights, stays, train and experiences), with Tanzania as the initial market. The legal assessment for each mode and country is of varying completeness; train and experiences are first-class product scope, while their jurisdiction-specific supplier/licensing requirements still need validation.  
**Use:** Regulatory issue-spotting and launch planning, not formal legal advice.

## Evidence and status labels

- **Verified:** Supported by an official act, regulation, regulator notice or regulator service page reviewed for this installment.
- **Preliminary:** Official source exists but application to Travelar's precise facts is unresolved.
- **Unresolved:** Requires current written confirmation, a full legal review or documents not publicly available.
- **Not yet assessed:** Research for a later installment; not a finding that a rule does not apply.

## Core regulatory conclusions

1. **Bus e-ticketing requires an authorized pathway.** LATRA's 2024 Online Ticketing Regulations (Government Notice No. 23, dated 12 January 2024 in the instrument) define an online ticketing system as one tested and approved by LATRA. A person seeking to provide such a system must apply and meet the requirements. Operators/service providers and agents must use an approved system under the rules.
2. **System ownership and ticket distribution are distinct roles.** A system owner seeking LATRA approval has eligibility, testing, cyber, continuity, operational support, and ongoing duties. A service provider that is different from the system owner must contract with the system owner. The regulations contemplate agents, but Travelar must get LATRA to confirm whether the exact proposed consumer marketplace, OIMS, Travelar-agent and call-centre flows are classified as an agent, a provider, a system owner, or some combination.
3. **The tax-system integration is a prerequisite, not a later enhancement.** The 2024 LATRA rules include connection with the electronic revenue-management system and capability to connect with LATRA's Main Online Ticketing System and other required systems. LATRA's 2025 notice says approval depends on connection to TRA EFDMS and the central CeTS system. Obtain technical and fiscal specifications directly.
4. **PDPC registration and transfer assessment must precede live passenger data.** PDPC says a person must not collect or process personal data without registration as a controller or processor. PDPC also says entities intending to transfer personal data outside Tanzania must apply for a cross-border transfer permit and demonstrate adequate safeguards.
5. **Business and sector licensing need a formal business-activity classification.** The Tanzania National Business Portal states that businesses require a valid business licence and lists BRELA registration, TIN, business premises, sector permission and the relevant licence as prerequisites. It identifies Tourist Agency Licence coverage for travel agents/air ticketing and separately lists marine transport, shipping and related businesses requiring sector-specific licensing. Whether the bus/ferry ticket marketplace by itself is a “travel agent” or “tourist agent” must be confirmed in writing; flight distribution makes the issue more direct.
6. **Do not become an unlicensed payments intermediary by accident.** The Bank of Tanzania is the payment-systems regulator under the National Payment Systems Act, 2015 and licensing regulations. Using a licensed PSP as a merchant is not the same as operating a regulated payment system, but custody, aggregation, wallet/stored value, onward funds transfer or settlement functionality may change the analysis. Obtain a BoT/counsel determination for actual money flows.

## Direct answers to the eight first-report questions

### 1. What must Travelar satisfy to launch a bus and ferry marketplace?

Minimum verified/likely conditions are: valid OuterBlocks company and business licence scope; the correct LATRA-approved ticketing pathway; a provider/operator agreement; TRA EFDMS/CeTS capability and tested ticket/data flows where required; lawful customer money flow using authorized payment services; PDPC registration before processing personal data; lawful cross-border transfer approvals before exporting personal data; transparent online-consumer disclosures; and signed ferry/operator arrangements validated by the relevant maritime regulator. A travel-agency licence is a strong issue to resolve early, especially before enabling air ticketing or packaged tourism services.

### 2. What is LATRA's role in Safari Tiketi?

LATRA is the land-transport regulator and the central e-ticketing/CeTS oversight operator described in its regulations. The rules define the “Main Online Ticketing System” as the principal system for handling and controlling online ticket issuance, integrating provider systems for information and record monitoring under LATRA. The public passenger portal is `https://tiketi.latra.go.tz/`. LATRA also publishes fares, operator/service information and notices about approved systems. It is important to distinguish the regulator's central system from a commercial provider such as Busbora or OTAPP.

### 3. What approvals/contracts/licences may be required?

- OuterBlocks: BRELA/company status, TIN, appropriate business licence, appropriate Tourist Agency Licence if the activity classification requires it, PDPC controller/processor registration, supplier/PSP contracts, and potentially LATRA's online-ticketing system approval or a formal agent/distribution agreement with an approved system owner.
- Bus operators: LATRA transport/operator/vehicle/route licences and use of an authorized e-ticketing platform; specific operating obligations remain with the operator.
- Ferry operators: appropriate vessel/operator/route/safety approvals under the applicable maritime regime; TASAC and potentially Zanzibar authority confirmation for Zanzibar-facing routes.
- PSPs: the selected PSP must hold the required BoT authorization for the actual service; whether OuterBlocks needs an independent payment authorization depends on the real money flow and services it performs.

### 4. What data-protection/localization rules are verified?

PDPC registration before personal-data processing and a permit-based framework for personal data transferred out of Tanzania are verified. The law provides data-subject rights and regulations include DPIA requirements for higher-risk processing. The reviewed public sources do not prove a universal Tanzania-only storage rule for every data class. The exact permit, lawful basis, security safeguards, retention and cross-border access must be evaluated per data flow. Do not send live manifests to offshore systems merely because the vendor is reputable.

### 5. Who are the current competitors and systems?

The established ecosystem includes LATRA's Safari Tiketi/CeTS central system; the connected provider set published by LATRA in October 2026; marketplace/operator tooling such as Busbora; OTAPP's ticketing and broader entertainment/travel catalogue; and operator direct channels such as Azam Marine's booking website. Direct operator systems beyond publicly visible materials have not yet been inventoried individually.

### 6. What are documented capabilities and potential gaps?

Busbora publicly claims consumer bookings, operator-branded websites, counter ticketing, mobile money and SMS integration, and operator management; OTAPP publicly claims online/offline ticketing and multiple ticket categories. LATRA/CeTS provides central monitoring and connects provider systems. A clear market-wide customer pain point, platform reliability comparison, operator satisfaction score, API quality gap or share figure has **not** been verified. The opportunity hypotheses are multi-mode trip discovery, consistent guest/account booking, contractual integrations across existing systems, transparent commission attribution, strong reconciliation, and low-connectivity/assisted channels—but they require interviews and workflow testing.

### 7. What is a launch blocker versus a commercial agreement versus a regulator question?

- **Blocker:** lawful LATRA path and ticket issuance rights; correct OuterBlocks legal/business registrations; PDPC registration; cross-border permit where relevant; ferry supplier and licensing authorization; approved PSP/funds-flow model; verified inventory and refund responsibilities.
- **Commercial agreement:** operator inventory, schedule/fare accuracy, revenue shares, commission attribution, service levels, refunds, settlement timing, marketing rights, API/data access, data portability and dispute handling.
- **Regulator confirmation:** system-owner versus agent classification, current LATRA amendments and approval pathway, exact EFDMS/CeTS integration, ferry marketplace role/TASAC permissions, Zanzibar maritime regime, tourist agency licence applicability, BoT classification of customer-money flows, cross-border transfer requirements and current fees/timelines.

### 8. Which sources are most authoritative and what should be asked?

Primary sources are the LATRA 2024 regulation and current amendment notices; current LATRA approved-provider notices and the October 2026 Safari Tiketi statement; PDPC Act/Regulations and service pages; the Bank of Tanzania Act/regulations and written guidance; the Office of the Attorney General's revised legislation library; the Tanzania National Business Portal; TASAC and Merchant Shipping legislation; TCRA numbering rules; and official provider API/contracts rather than third-party summaries. The regulator/provider questions are listed in [`31-open-questions-and-evidence-needed-before-launch.md`](31-open-questions-and-evidence-needed-before-launch.md).

## Practical Tanzania launch checklist

### Gate A — legal entity and scope
- [ ] Obtain current BRELA company extracts for both OuterBlocks Limited and OuterBlocks Destinations Limited; verify each exact registered name, status, directors, shareholding/relationship, registered office and business objects.
- [ ] Obtain TIN/tax registration evidence and confirm tax registrations that apply.
- [ ] Identify the correct Class A/Class B business licence and activity code with the business-licensing authority.
- [ ] Obtain written tourism/travel-agent activity classification; secure Tourist Agency Licence if applicable.
- [ ] Define precisely which services launch: bus-only, bus+ferry, or both; domestic-only versus cross-border; agency versus principal; funds collection/settlement role.

### Gate B — LATRA bus ticketing
- [ ] Review LATRA's 1 July 2026 amendments to the relevant operator-licensing rules; the published notice does not list the 2024 Online Ticketing Regulations. Ask LATRA whether any separate later instrument has amended online-ticketing requirements or fees.
- [ ] Obtain current approved-provider/certificate list and separate “connected,” “approved” and “production authorized” statuses.
- [ ] Decide system owner versus authorized provider/agent route and obtain written LATRA confirmation.
- [ ] Obtain current EFDMS/CeTS technical integration, certification, fee and security assessment specifications.
- [ ] If own system: gather company certificate, valid business licence, office evidence, tax clearance, TIN and other required documents; map application fee, certificate fee, renewal, support and recurring fees.
- [ ] Agree test cycles, vulnerability assessment, disaster-recovery evidence, uptime, scheduled maintenance, outage notification and data-retention expectations.
- [ ] Agree contracts with operators/system owner covering responsibility for ticket issuance, fare/fee display, refunds, rescheduling, manifests, records and service continuity.

### Gate C — ferry routes
- [ ] Obtain written TASAC classification of Travelar's ticket-distribution model.
- [ ] Verify every ferry operator, vessel, route, timetable, service class/capacity, passenger eligibility and refund/cancellation rule against operator records.
- [ ] For Zanzibar-facing routes, obtain advice and written confirmation on Zanzibar maritime requirements and which authority approves each part of the service.
- [ ] Confirm manifest submission format/timing, ticket validation, substitute vessel, delay and interruption procedures.

### Gate D — money, tax and settlement
- [ ] Draw a funds-flow diagram for each booking mode and name the merchant of record, PSP, supplier, settlement account owner and party bearing chargeback/refund liability.
- [ ] Validate each PSP's current licence, country/currency support, merchant onboarding, fees, settlement, refunds and webhooks.
- [ ] Obtain BoT/counsel determination if Travelar aggregates, holds, splits, transfers or stores funds beyond ordinary merchant acceptance.
- [ ] Confirm VAT/tax invoice/receipt treatment with TRA/tax adviser for operator fare, marketplace commission, customer service fees and supplier commission/markup.
- [ ] Avoid customer payment surcharges until legally permissible, scheme-compliant and contractually allowed for the exact method.
- [ ] Reconcile successful payments against confirmed ticket/booking IDs before issue; define late-success/expired-hold remediation.

### Gate E — privacy and security
- [ ] Register OuterBlocks as the appropriate PDPC controller and/or processor, resolving document requirements for a newly formed company.
- [ ] Name and empower a DPO/contact as required by PDPC process.
- [ ] Complete data inventory, purposes/lawful bases, privacy notice, processing records, retention schedule, rights process, processor terms and DPIA where required.
- [ ] Complete all cross-border transfer applications/permits before transferring personal data outside Tanzania; inventory offshore support access and backups.
- [ ] Verify payment-card scope/PCI obligations with PSP and security adviser; Travelar should not store card data unless explicitly justified and secured.
- [ ] Produce tested incident response and recovery plans, with role ownership and evidence-preservation process.

### Gate F — customer and operator launch readiness
- [ ] Publish accurate legal entity/contact, full price and fee disclosures, service description, booking-review/correction, terms, cancellation/refund rules and complaint contacts.
- [ ] Implement Kiswahili and English e-ticket flows if Travelar operates the authorized system; include all mandated ticket fields and QR/verification.
- [ ] Provide support and escalation routes, including relevant toll-free contact obligation if Travelar becomes system owner.
- [ ] Test operator/cashier sales, cash payment recording, fare matching, schedule updates, passenger list exports, cancellation/refund, offline boarding and reconciliation.
- [ ] Complete regulator/provider/UAT sign-off and controlled production activation before taking public bookings.

## Kenya and other countries

This file is intentionally **Tanzania-first**. Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Ethiopia, Ghana, Nigeria and South Africa have not been legally assessed to the level required for launch. Reuse only the general process (legal entity and activity classification, transport authorization, payment-service role, data-controller/processor role, localization/cross-border map, supplier agreements and consumer disclosures). Do not reuse Tanzania-specific LATRA/CeTS assumptions in another country. Kenya needs its own NTSA/rail/maritime/aviation, ODPC, KRA, CBK, Communications Authority and consumer-law research before market activation.
