## Evidence classification summary

- **Verified through primary documentation:** public product/API capabilities and named booking channels as described above.
- **Supported by multiple primary sources:** broad conclusion that GDS, lodging and experiences transactions require differentiating technical access from contractual/production authorization.
- **Preliminary:** recommended priorities and product architecture implications.
- **Unknown / requires direct confirmation:** fees, production access for OuterBlocks Limited, exact inventory/markets, commercial contracts, supplier service levels and legal classification for each activity.
