## Evidence classification

- **Primary, official regulatory/statistical evidence:** LATRA's official CeTS posts and NBS Statistical Abstract.
- **Primary but commercial self-description:** provider/operator public websites and terms. Useful for advertised capability and available workflows, not independent quality claims.
- **Preliminary inference:** Travelar opportunities based on visible market structures; must be tested with operators, written supplier access and measured pilot performance.
- **Unknown:** API access, production rights, fees, operator system deployments, service-level performance, customer sentiment, data portability and contractual obligations unless expressly established by a source/contract.
