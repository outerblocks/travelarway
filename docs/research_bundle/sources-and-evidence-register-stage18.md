# Sources and Evidence Register — Stage 18: Destination Discovery and Travel Guides

**Research date:** 24 October 2026. This register supports homepage/content architecture and source-governance decisions; it does not certify every destination's current requirements or grant Travelar commercial API/republication rights.

1. **IATA Travel Centre.** https://www.iata.org/en/travel-centre — traveller-facing lookup of passport, visa and health requirements; source describes its use of official source material. Does not establish API, bulk use, cache or republication rights for Travelar.
2. **IATA Travelers Questions.** https://www.iata.org/en/travelers/ — says entry requirements depend on traveller details, may change, and satisfying documentation requirements does not guarantee admission. General information for the traveller audience, not legal advice or a guarantee.
3. **IATA Timatic Solutions.** https://www.iata.org/en/services/compliance/timatic — describes government/airline sources, validation and update processes. Access/use for Travelar requires separate contract, permissions and commercial review.
4. **Tanzania Immigration Services, visa guidelines.** https://visa.immigration.go.tz/guidelines — primary source for Tanzania visa application and eligibility procedures. Any page should capture access/review date and exact applicability; conditions may change.
5. **Tanzania Immigration Services eVisa portal.** https://visa.immigration.go.tz/ — official entry point for current instructions/application. Do not link users to lookalike visa application services.
6. **UK FCDO Foreign travel checklist.** https://www.gov.uk/guidance/foreign-travel-checklist — current official guidance (page shows last updated 5 June 2026 in this research) on checking entry, safety, health, documents, insurance and local laws. Its scope is UK travelers; do not apply its rules to all passport holders.
7. **GOV.UK Tanzania entry requirements.** https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/tanzania/entry-requirements — example of nationality-scoped advice for UK travellers and destination-specific conditions. Must not be generalized to all travellers and must be rechecked on publication.

## Classification and limitations

- **Verified — primary source:** the cited public page exists and states the cited general behavior/policy within its scope.
- **Proposed product control:** canonical destination IDs, source metadata, visible review timestamps, content lifecycle and a stale-content policy. These are Travelar requirements proposed from product-risk reasoning, not government mandates.
- **Unknown / direct confirmation required:** licensed travel-document data feed access, API/display/caching rights, per-destination current visa rules for all traveller categories, and staffing/service capacity to keep information current.
- **No-go for unsupported claims:** do not publish categorical visa/arrival claims without applicable, current evidence; do not imply a public information page authorizes commercial integration.
