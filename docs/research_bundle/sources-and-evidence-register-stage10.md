## Evidence labels

- **Verified through provider/regulator primary documentation:** what official public pages expressly publish.
- **Provider claim requiring substantiation:** uptime, Tier certification, security, availability and operational descriptions without independent evidence reviewed.
- **Unknown:** contracted terms, customer-specific performance, processor/backup locations, RPO/RTO, referenceable incident outcomes and total cost.
- **Decision required:** provider selection, placement of production and DR, managed versus self-managed operations.
