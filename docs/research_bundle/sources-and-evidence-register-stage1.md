## Evidence limits and citation conventions

- The strongest findings in this installment rely on primary sources (regulator, statute, official government portals and providers' own sites).
- Provider pages are evidence of marketed features, not independent proof of performance, uptime, production supplier rights, market share or customer satisfaction.
- A public regulator statement that systems are “connected” is not treated as a substitute for a current approval certificate, API spec or contract.
- LATRA's 30 June 2026 notice (effective 1 July) lists amendments to private-hire, freight and passenger-vehicle licensing rules, not the 2024 Online Ticketing Regulations. That notice is relevant to operator licensing but does not itself evidence a change to the e-ticketing fee schedule; any separate later e-ticketing amendment or fee circular remains to be confirmed directly.
- This source register is a researched starting point, not an exhaustive case-law search or formal Tanzanian legal opinion.
